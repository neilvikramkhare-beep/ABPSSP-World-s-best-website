About ABPSSP

The Akhil Bharatiya Purva Sainik Seva Parishad (ABPSSP) is a premier organization dedicated to the welfare of Ex-Servicemen from the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force.

Our History

Formed by veterans, the Parishad has been active in addressing the grievances and promoting the well-being of former soldiers across India.

Our Mission

To unite all ex-servicemen on a single platform, ensure their dignity, and utilize their disciplined force for the betterment of society.

Akhil Bharatiya Purva Sainik Seva Parishad

Empowering veterans, supporting families, and contributing to nation-building.
