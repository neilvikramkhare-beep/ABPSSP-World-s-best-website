SPARSH Pension System Update

Posted on August 20, 2026

Important information regarding life certificate submission for pensioners migrated to SPARSH.

The System for Pension Administration (Raksha) - SPARSH is a comprehensive web-based comprehensive pension sanction and payment system. All Defense Pensioners who have been migrated to SPARSH are required to submit their Annual Life Certificate through the SPARSH portal or via Jeevan Pramaan.

Key Deadlines: The last date for submitting the Annual Life Certificate without disruption to the pension is November 30, 2026.

How to Submit:

  • Login to the SPARSH Portal using your credentials.
  • Visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) with your Aadhaar Card.
  • Use the Jeevan Pramaan Face App on your Android smartphone.

If you face any issues, please contact your nearest ABPSSP district office, and our volunteers will assist you with the process.
