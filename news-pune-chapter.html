New Chapter Opened in Pune

Posted on August 15, 2026

The Parishad is proud to announce the inauguration of a new district office to serve the large veteran community in Pune.

Pune, with its rich military history and significant presence of Ex-Servicemen from the Southern Command, has long required a dedicated chapter to address the specific needs of the local veteran community.

The inauguration ceremony took place on Independence Day and was attended by several distinguished veterans and local community leaders. The new office will serve as a hub for:

  • Pension and ECHS grievance redressal
  • Second career counseling for retiring personnel
  • Community outreach programs and social initiatives

Office Address: ABPSSP Pune Chapter, 45 Cantonment Road, Camp, Pune - 411001.

We invite all Ex-Servicemen residing in and around Pune to visit the office and join the Parishad in our mission to serve the nation even after retirement.
