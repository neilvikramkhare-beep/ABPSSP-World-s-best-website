Honoring Our Heroes, Serving Our Nation

Welcome to the Akhil Bharatiya Purva Sainik Seva Parishad. We are dedicated to the welfare, resettlement, and empowerment of Ex-Servicemen of the Indian Armed Forces.

Join the Parishad

Our Objectives

Welfare

Providing assistance to veterans, widows, and dependents through healthcare and financial support programs.

Resettlement

Facilitating second career opportunities and skill development for retiring armed forces personnel.

Nation Building

Channelling the discipline and expertise of veterans towards social work and national development.

Akhil Bharatiya Purva Sainik Seva Parishad

Empowering veterans, supporting families, and contributing to nation-building.
